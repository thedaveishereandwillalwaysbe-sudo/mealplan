☀️ SUNDAY BATCH COOK (~90 min total, 20 min active)
Batch Roast Chicken Thighs (2 kg) — DREO ChefMaker ⏱ 40 min total (2 batches) • 10 min prep

Ingredients (Week A: Garlic & Lemon)

  • 2 kg skinless boneless chicken thigh fillets
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • Juice of 1 lemon + zest
  • 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried oregano or mixed herbs

Week B Variant: Smoked Paprika

  • Same chicken. Replace lemon/garlic with: 2 tbsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp cumin, 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp salt.

DREO ChefMaker Method

  1. Toss chicken with oil and seasoning in a bowl. No need to preheat.
  2. Fill the DREO water tank to the MAX line (this enables steam injection for juicier results).
  3. Place ~1 kg of thighs in the cooking tray in a single layer (half the batch — 6-quart capacity means 2 batches).
  4. Insert the cook probe into the thickest thigh.
  5. Select Chef Mode → Poultry → Chicken Thigh. The machine sets its own temperature staging and steam levels.
  6. Press start. Cook until probe signals done — typically 18–22 minutes. The steam + convection gives a crispy exterior with juicy interior.
  7. Remove batch 1 to a plate. Repeat with batch 2.
  8. Rest 5 minutes. Slice or shred. Portion into 6 adult containers + reserve for lunches.

Oven Fallback (if ChefMaker is occupied)

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C / 180°C fan / Gas 6. Spread in single layer in roasting tin.
  2. Roast 35–40 minutes until golden and internal temp reaches 75°C.
💡 Run the rice on the hob and eggs in a pan simultaneously. The slow cooker Bolognese should already be on from 9am. All four elements run in parallel — 15 minutes of active work produces the entire week's food.
Per 150g portion: ~263 kcal • P: 40g • F: 11g • C: 0g
Brown Rice (large batch, 300g dry) ⏱ 25 min • passive

Ingredients

  • 300g brown basmati rice
  • 600ml water
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Rinse rice in cold water until water runs clear (removes excess starch).
  2. Add rice, water and salt to a medium saucepan. Bring to boil.
  3. Reduce to lowest heat, cover with tight-fitting lid. Cook 25 minutes — do not lift the lid.
  4. Remove from heat, keep lid on, rest 5 minutes.
  5. Fluff with fork. Cool completely before refrigerating (important for food safety).
💡 For egg fried rice (Week B Monday): use this leftover rice cold from the fridge. Cold rice fries better — less sticky.
Per 100g cooked: ~141 kcal • P: 3g • F: 1g • C: 32g
Hard-Boiled Eggs (6) ⏱ 12 min • passive

Method

  1. Place 6 eggs in a single layer in a saucepan. Cover with cold water by 2cm.
  2. Bring to a rolling boil over high heat.
  3. Once boiling, reduce heat slightly and boil for exactly 10 minutes.
  4. Transfer immediately to a bowl of cold water (or ice water). Cool for 5 minutes.
  5. Peel or store unpeeled in fridge — they keep 5 days in shell.
💡 The cold water plunge stops cooking and makes peeling much easier. Older eggs peel better than fresh ones.
Per egg: ~78 kcal • P: 6g • F: 5g • C: 0.6g
Turkey Mince Bolognese — Slow Cooker (Week A) ⏱ 8 min active • 5–6 hrs slow cook

Ingredients

  • 1 kg turkey mince (2 × 500g packs)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion, finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 × 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)

Slow Cooker Method

  1. Heat oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
  2. Add onion, cook 3 minutes. Add turkey mince, break up with wooden spoon, cook 5 minutes until browned. Add garlic, stir 30 seconds.
  3. Transfer everything to the slow cooker. Add tinned tomatoes, tomato purée, herbs, bay leaf. Stir well.
  4. Set to LOW. Cook for 5–6 hours (or HIGH for 3–4 hours).
  5. Season to taste. Remove bay leaf.
  6. Portion: 3 adult containers for Tue/Wed/Thu dinners + 2–3 child containers (smaller portions).

Why slow cooker instead of stovetop?

  • 8 minutes active time vs 30 minutes stovetop — set it at 9am after the anchor session, it's done by 3pm.
  • Deeper flavour — the long, slow simmer breaks down the tomatoes and melds the herbs better than a quick stovetop cook.
  • Zero monitoring — no stirring, no burning risk. You're doing family time while it cooks.
💡 Week B variant — Turkey Chilli: add 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chilli powder (mild), 1 drained tin kidney beans at step 3. Same slow cooker method. For the child: portion out before adding chilli flakes.
Per adult portion (~250g): ~280 kcal • P: 35g • F: 9g • C: 12g
🌙 MONDAY EVENING PREP (~25 min)
Overnight Oats Jars × 3 ⏱ 8 min total

Per Jar

  • 80g rolled oats (Essential Porridge Oats)
  • 200ml skimmed milk
  • 1 scoop (25g) whey protein powder
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • Topping: handful blueberries (Week A) or sliced banana (Week B)

Method

  1. Add oats, milk, whey and chia seeds to a 500ml jar. Stir or shake with lid on.
  2. Add fruit topping on top (don't stir in — keeps texture).
  3. Seal lid. Refrigerate. Ready in 4+ hours (overnight is ideal).
  4. Eat cold straight from jar on the train. No heating needed.
💡 Build all 3 jars in one go — line them up, assembly-line style. Total: 8 minutes for 3 jars.
Per jar: ~420 kcal • P: 38g • F: 8g • C: 48g

🍳 FRIDAY DINNER (Roderick cooks, 30–40 min)
Salmon + Broccoli + Sweet Potato — DREO ChefMaker (Week A Fri) ⏱ 25 min total

Ingredients (3 adults)

  • 4 salmon fillets (use 3 for adults, keep 1 for Saturday lunch)
  • 2 heads broccoli, cut into florets
  • 2–3 medium sweet potatoes, cubed (2cm pieces)
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Lemon wedges to serve

DREO ChefMaker Method — Two Stages

  1. Stage 1 — Veg: Toss sweet potato cubes and broccoli florets in 2 tbsp oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder.
  2. Place in ChefMaker cooking tray. Select Classic Mode → Air Fry → 190°C → 15 minutes.
  3. Shake basket halfway through. Remove when golden and tender. Set aside (cover with foil to keep warm).
  4. Stage 2 — Salmon: Fill the DREO water tank. Place salmon fillets in the tray, skin side down. Season with salt, pepper, drizzle of oil.
  5. Insert cook probe into the thickest fillet.
  6. Select Chef Mode → Seafood → Salmon. The machine manages temperature and steam automatically.
  7. Cook until probe signals done — typically 10–12 minutes. The steam keeps the salmon incredibly moist while the convection gives a light crust on top.
  8. Serve salmon on the veg. Squeeze lemon over everything.
💡 Total hands-on time: 5 minutes (cutting veg + seasoning). The ChefMaker does the rest. No oven preheat, no temperature watching. The probe means perfect salmon every single time — no more guessing if it's done.
Per adult serving: ~520 kcal • P: 38g • F: 22g • C: 40g
Salmon en Papillote — Oven only, not ChefMaker (Week B Fri) ⏱ 25 min

Why oven, not ChefMaker?

  • The parchment parcel technique relies on trapped steam inside a sealed packet. The ChefMaker's convection fan would blow the parchment open, defeating the purpose. This one stays in the oven.

Ingredients (3 adults)

  • 3–4 salmon fillets
  • 1 lemon, thinly sliced
  • Fresh dill or thyme (a few sprigs per fillet, or 1 tsp dried herbs)
  • 1 tbsp butter or olive oil per fillet
  • Salt and pepper
  • 3 large squares of baking parchment (~30×40cm each)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C / 180°C fan / Gas 6.
  2. Place each salmon fillet in the centre of a parchment square.
  3. Season with salt and pepper. Add a knob of butter, lemon slices, and herbs on top.
  4. Fold parchment over the salmon. Crimp and fold edges tightly to seal — the packet should be airtight.
  5. Place packets on a baking tray. Bake 15–18 minutes.
  6. The packet will puff up when ready. Serve on plates — open at the table. Be careful of hot steam.
💡 Sides: air fry the sweet potato and broccoli in the ChefMaker (Classic → Air Fry, 190°C, 15 min) while the oven preheats and salmon bakes. Both appliances running in parallel = dinner in 25 minutes.
Per fillet: ~350 kcal • P: 34g • F: 22g • C: 1g (plus sides)
🍲 SATURDAY DINNER
Chicken Thighs — Lemon Herb — DREO ChefMaker (Week A Sat) ⏱ 25 min

Ingredients (3 adults + child portion)

  • 6–8 chicken thigh fillets (from second 1kg pack, or leftover from Sunday)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice and zest of 1 lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried thyme or rosemary
  • Salt and pepper
  • Green beans (2 bags, trimmed) and brown rice to serve

DREO ChefMaker Method

  1. Mix oil, lemon juice, zest, garlic, herbs in a bowl. Toss chicken to coat.
  2. Fill the DREO water tank. Place chicken in cooking tray, single layer.
  3. Insert cook probe into the thickest thigh.
  4. Select Chef Mode → Poultry → Chicken Thigh. Press start.
  5. Cook until probe signals done — typically 18–22 minutes.
  6. Meanwhile: boil green beans on the hob for 4–5 minutes. Reheat batch rice in ChefMaker after chicken is done (Classic → Reheat, 160°C, 5 min) or microwave.
  7. Serve. Child gets a smaller portion with peas instead of green beans if preferred.
💡 If making fresh rather than using Sunday batch leftovers, this is the exact same ChefMaker method as Sunday — just with different seasoning. The probe eliminates all guesswork.
Per adult serving (2 thighs + rice + beans): ~480 kcal • P: 45g • F: 15g • C: 35g
Slow Cooker Red Lentil Dhal (Week B Saturday) ⏱ 10 min prep • 5–6 hrs slow cook

Ingredients (3 adults + child portion)

  • 300g red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 × 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 × 400ml tin coconut milk (full fat)
  • 500ml vegetable stock (1 stock cube + water)
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes (leave out for child-mild version)
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander to serve (optional)

Method

  1. Rinse red lentils under cold water until water runs clear.
  2. Add all ingredients except coconut milk to the slow cooker. Stir well.
  3. Cook on LOW for 5–6 hours or HIGH for 3–4 hours. Lentils should be completely soft and broken down.
  4. Add coconut milk in the last 30 minutes of cooking. Stir through.
  5. Season with salt. The dhal should be thick and creamy — add a splash of water if too thick.
  6. Serve over brown rice with fresh coriander on top if you have it.
💡 Set the slow cooker at lunchtime on Saturday (~12pm). It's ready by 6pm with zero effort. The child version: portion out before adding chilli flakes. Leftovers freeze brilliantly — portion into containers for easy future meals.
Per adult serving (~350g): ~380 kcal • P: 18g • F: 14g • C: 42g
Egg Fried Rice (Week B Monday) ⏱ 15 min

Ingredients (3 adults + child portion)

  • Leftover cold brown rice from Sunday batch (~500g cooked)
  • 6–9 eggs (2–3 per adult)
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil (or olive oil)
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated (optional)
  • 2 spring onions, sliced (optional)

Method

  1. Heat oil in a large wok or frying pan over high heat.
  2. Crack eggs directly into pan. Scramble roughly for 1–2 minutes until just set. Remove to a plate.
  3. Add a splash more oil. Add cold rice to the hot pan. Stir-fry for 3–4 minutes until rice is hot and starting to crisp.
  4. Add frozen peas directly (they'll defrost in 2 minutes from the heat).
  5. Return eggs to pan. Add soy sauce and ginger. Toss everything together for 1–2 minutes.
  6. Serve immediately. Top with spring onions if using.
💡 The key: cold rice + very hot pan = proper fried rice. Freshly cooked rice goes mushy. This is why Sunday's batch rice is perfect here.
Per adult serving: ~420 kcal • P: 22g • F: 15g • C: 45g
👶 CHILD QUICK MEALS (Partner heats, 10–15 min max)
Bolognese over Penne + Cheddar ⏱ 12 min
  1. Boil kettle. Cook penne pasta in salted boiling water — 10–12 minutes per pack instructions.
  2. Reheat a pre-portioned container of turkey Bolognese/chilli sauce in the microwave — 2–3 minutes, stir halfway.
  3. Drain pasta. Top with sauce. Grate cheddar over the top.
Sausages + Peas + Mash — DREO ChefMaker ⏱ 15 min (no preheat)

DREO ChefMaker Method

  1. Place sausages in ChefMaker tray. Select Classic Mode → Air Fry → 190°C → 14 minutes. No preheat needed — press start immediately.
  2. Turn sausages halfway through (at 7 min) for even browning.
  3. While sausages cook: peel and chop 1–2 potatoes. Boil in salted water 15 minutes until tender. Drain, mash with a knob of butter and splash of milk.
  4. Boil frozen peas for 3 minutes. Drain. Serve alongside.
💡 For the partner: this is genuinely a "put sausages in, press button, boil potatoes" job. No oven preheat saves 10 minutes vs the old method. Total hands-on time: 5 minutes.
Fish Fingers + Peas — DREO ChefMaker ⏱ 12 min (no preheat)

DREO ChefMaker Method

  1. Place fish fingers in ChefMaker tray in a single layer. Select Classic Mode → Air Fry → 200°C → 10 minutes.
  2. No preheat needed. No turning needed — the convection cooks evenly.
  3. Boil frozen peas for 3 minutes while fish fingers cook. Serve with ketchup.
💡 Crispier than oven-baked, 8 minutes faster (no preheat). Add sweet potato wedges alongside if wanted: toss in oil, add to tray with fish fingers, add 5 min extra (Classic → Air Fry → 190°C → 15 min total).
Mac & Cheese (from box) ⏱ 12 min
  1. Follow pack instructions. Typically: boil pasta 8–10 minutes.
  2. Drain. Stir in the cheese sauce packet with a splash of milk and a knob of butter.
  3. Optionally stir in frozen peas (add to pasta water in last 2 minutes of cooking).
Bacon Penne + Cheddar ⏱ 14 min
  1. Cook penne pasta — 10–12 minutes.
  2. While pasta cooks: chop 2–3 bacon rashers into small pieces. Fry in a dry pan over medium heat 4–5 minutes until crispy.
  3. Drain pasta, reserving 2 tbsp pasta water. Toss pasta with bacon, pasta water, and a generous handful of grated cheddar. Stir until cheese melts into a simple sauce.
Beans on Toast / Instant Noodles ⏱ 5 min

Beans on Toast

  1. Toast 1–2 slices of bread. Heat beans in a saucepan for 3–4 minutes (or microwave 2 minutes, stir, 1 minute more).
  2. Pour beans over toast. Add grated cheddar if wanted. Serve with fruit alongside.

Instant Noodles (emergency option)

  1. Boil kettle. Cook noodles per pack instructions (typically 4 minutes).
  2. Add a handful of frozen peas in the last minute. Drain most of the water.
  3. Stir in the flavouring sachet. Serve.
💡 These are the 5:45pm "nobody planned anything" options. No guilt. Add fruit (apple, grapes, banana) on the side and it's a fine child dinner.
🍳 BREAKFAST METHODS
Poached Eggs (for Babs) ⏱ 6 min
  1. Fill a medium saucepan with water ~8cm deep. Bring to a gentle simmer — small bubbles, not a rolling boil.
  2. Add 1 tbsp white vinegar (helps the white hold together). Optional but recommended.
  3. Crack each egg into a small cup or ramekin first.
  4. Create a gentle swirl in the water with a spoon. Slide the egg in from the cup.
  5. Cook for 3 minutes for a runny yolk, 4 minutes for medium.
  6. Remove with a slotted spoon. Rest briefly on kitchen paper to drain. Season and serve.
💡 Fresh eggs poach better (the white is tighter). For 2 eggs: do them one at a time, 30 seconds apart, in the same pan.
Per egg: ~78 kcal • P: 6g • F: 5g • C: 0.6g
Bialetti Moka Pot — 05:00 Coffee Routine ⏱ 6 min (prep night before)

Night Before (1 minute)

  1. Fill the Bialetti base with cold water to just below the safety valve.
  2. Fill the filter basket with ground coffee (Essential Italian Style or any medium-fine grind). Level off — don't tamp or press down.
  3. Place the basket in the base. Leave the top unscrewed on the counter beside the stove.

05:00 Morning (5 minutes, hands-free)

  1. Screw the top on firmly (hand-tight, no tools).
  2. Place on hob over medium heat. Leave the lid up so you can see when coffee starts flowing.
  3. Wait ~4–5 minutes. Coffee will start gurgling up into the top chamber.
  4. When the flow turns from dark to pale honey colour and you hear a hissing/spluttering sound — remove from heat immediately. Don't let it boil.
  5. Pour. Black, no milk. Ready before the 05:15 training session.
💡 The gurgle is your alarm clock. Prep the night before and the entire routine is: walk to kitchen, screw top on, turn on gas, get dressed, pour coffee, train. Caffeine cut-off: 13:00 — the Bialetti is a morning-only tool. One 3-cup pot = ~130–150mg caffeine (strong double espresso equivalent).
♻️ REHEATING — DREO ChefMaker vs Microwave
Reheating Batch Meals (Tue/Wed/Thu Dinners) — DREO ChefMaker ⏱ 6–8 min

Why ChefMaker instead of microwave?

  • Microwaved batch chicken goes rubbery and uneven. The ChefMaker's convection reheats evenly and restores crispiness to the chicken surface.
  • Takes 3 minutes longer than microwave but the difference between "sad leftovers" and "proper dinner" is this step.

DREO ChefMaker Method

  1. Take pre-portioned container from fridge. Transfer chicken + rice + veg to ChefMaker tray. Spread evenly — don't pile.
  2. Select Classic Mode → Reheat → 160°C → 6–8 minutes.
  3. No need to cover or add water. The convection reheats and re-crisps simultaneously.
  4. Check food is piping hot throughout before serving.
💡 For the partner reheating child's dinner at 5:30pm: use the same ChefMaker method for pre-portioned child Bolognese. Or microwave is fine for the child's pasta-based meals — the sauce protects the texture. ChefMaker is most valuable for protein + rice + veg combinations.
